Safety guide

What to do at each stage of a typhoon.

Before

  • Prepare your supplies.
  • Charge phones and power banks.
  • Secure windows, doors, and loose objects outside.
  • Know where your family will go if you need to evacuate.

During

  • Stay indoors, away from windows.
  • Listen to a radio or official updates.
  • Avoid floodwater and unplug appliances if water enters.
  • Evacuate only when authorities say it is safe to move.

After

  • Check for hazards like fallen wires and weak structures.
  • Stay out of flooded areas.
  • Boil or treat water if you are unsure it is safe.
  • Check on family and neighbors.

Wind signal guide

PAGASA uses Tropical Cyclone Wind Signals 1 to 5. A higher number means stronger winds and less time to prepare.
