About the system

Why we made Typhoon Safety Check and who it helps.

The problem

  • Typhoons can bring strong winds, heavy rain, flooding, and landslides.
  • Some people do not know which supplies to prepare or which safety steps to follow.
  • Emergency information is hard to find when people need it quickly.
  • Lack of preparation puts individuals and families at greater risk.

How the system helps

1

Prepare

Review a checklist of important emergency supplies.

2

Stay safe

Read safety reminders for before, during, and after a typhoon.

3

Track your checklist

Mark prepared items. Your progress stays saved in your browser.

Who it is for

  • Families and households
  • Students and teachers
  • Community members
  • Anyone who wants to prepare for typhoons

What it aims to do

  • Share basic typhoon preparedness information
  • Help users check emergency supplies
  • Explain safety actions before, during, and after a typhoon
  • Encourage users to prepare ahead of time

How it was built

Visual Studio Code

The code editor we used to create, organize, and edit our website files.

HTML and CSS

HTML gives the structure and content. CSS gives the colors, fonts, spacing, and layout.

JavaScript and localStorage

JavaScript handles the checklist actions. localStorage saves selections in the browser without a database.
