Emergency reminders

Small habits that make a big difference when a typhoon is near.

Always remember

  • Follow official local announcements and advisories.
  • Keep your emergency kit where everyone at home can reach it.
  • Review your checklist again before every typhoon season.
  • Save emergency numbers on your phone and on paper.

Where to get updates

  • PAGASA weather bulletins
  • Your local government and barangay announcements
  • NDRRMC advisories

Talk with your family

  • Agree on a meeting place.
  • Decide who will bring the emergency kit.
  • Plan how to check on elders and young children.
